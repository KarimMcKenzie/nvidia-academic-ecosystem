01 — Prioritize

Three universities. Three discovery problems.

Public data creates hypotheses. It does not establish institutional need or determine an NVIDIA engagement.

Selection logic: These institutions are not presented as priority targets. They were selected as contrasting public-data scenarios to pressure-test the operating model across a mature accelerated-computing environment, applied research and education, and multidisciplinary shared research services.

CALTECH

Mature technical ecosystem

Robotics/autonomy, advanced computing curriculum, mature GPU/CUDA environment.

Pressure test

Readiness ≠ unmet need.

Where could NVIDIA deepen, expand, or connect existing adoption — if an incremental gap exists?

AUBURN

Applied research + education

AU-CAICE AI/cyber, ICAMS, substantial HPC with existing NVIDIA GPU capacity.

Pressure test

Capability ≠ sufficiency.

Are workloads, access, curriculum, or adoption barriers limiting the intended academic outcome?

FIU

Shared research cores

Biostatistics, imaging, cell engineering, genomics and broad multidisciplinary R1 activity.

Pressure test

Follow the workflow.

Where does computation occur downstream, and is there a validated bottleneck NVIDIA can address?

Portfolio question: Which opportunities warrant deeper discovery — and what must be learned before NVIDIA commits resources?
